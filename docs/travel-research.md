# Travel Research — Baker Family Reunion 2027

**Where:** Sandwich, Massachusetts (Cape Cod — the first town over the Sagamore Bridge)
**When:** July 4th week, 2027

> Status: first-pass research, November 2026. Fares below are patterns and
> ranges, not quotes. Every route and price should be re-checked in
> Google Flights before anyone books.

---

## 1. The 2027 calendar matters

July 4, 2027 falls on a **Sunday**, so the federal holiday is observed
**Monday, July 5**. That shapes everything:

| Date | Day | What to expect for air travel |
|---|---|---|
| Tue Jun 29 | Tue | Quiet, normally priced. **Good arrival day** |
| Wed Jun 30 | Wed | Quiet, normally priced. **Good arrival day** |
| Thu Jul 1 | Thu | Peak outbound day. Expensive |
| Fri Jul 2 | Fri | Busiest and most expensive outbound day. Worst bridge traffic onto the Cape |
| Sat Jul 3 | Sat | Usually cheaper than Thu/Fri. **Acceptable arrival day** |
| **Sun Jul 4** | Sun | **The holiday.** Usually the cheapest day to fly, but you'd miss it |
| Mon Jul 5 | Mon (observed) | Heavy return day for the long weekend |
| Tue Jul 6 | Tue | Moderate. **OK departure day** |
| Wed Jul 7 | Wed | Normal pricing. **Best departure day** |
| Thu Jul 8 | Thu | Normal pricing. Good departure day |

The last time July 4 fell on a Sunday was 2021. That year Hopper and the
airlines named Thursday, July 1 and Friday, July 2 as the busiest days, and
KAYAK data put the Friday before the holiday about 25% above holiday-day
fares.

### Recommendation

- **Fly in:** Tuesday, June 29 or Wednesday, June 30. If someone can only
  do a short trip, Saturday, July 3 is the fallback.
- **Fly out:** Wednesday, July 7 (or Tuesday, July 6).
- **Avoid:** flying in Thursday, July 1 or Friday, July 2, and flying out
  Monday, July 5.

That gives a **Wed Jun 30 → Wed Jul 7** core window (one week) that covers
the whole holiday weekend on the Cape and skips the peak flying days in
both directions. Shifting a single day off a peak date usually saves about
$100–200 per ticket, or roughly 20%.

## 2. Which airport to fly into

| Airport | Drive to Sandwich | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Boston Logan (BOS)** | ~60 mi, 1–1.5 hr (much longer on a holiday Friday) | Most nonstops and best fares. **Default choice** |
| **Providence T.F. Green (PVD)** | ~65 mi, ~1.25 hr | Smaller and easier. Worth checking from Atlanta and from connecting itineraries |
| Hyannis (HYA) | ~15 mi | Very limited service (mainly small commuter flights). Not practical for most |

Sandwich is right at the Sagamore Bridge, so it's the easiest Cape town to
reach from either BOS or PVD. Nobody has to drive deep onto the Cape. Bridge
traffic is still the main bottleneck, and it's another reason to arrive
midweek.

## 3. City by city (flyers)

Routes are based on current published nonstops and should be confirmed for
summer 2027.

### Bend, OR — Redmond (RDM)
- **No nonstop to Boston.** RDM nonstops include Alaska (SEA, PDX, LAX, SFO,
  SAN, BUR), United (DEN, SFO, LAX), Delta (SEA, SLC) and American (PHX).
- **Best options:** one stop via **Seattle** (Alaska or Delta both fly
  SEA–BOS nonstop), **Denver**, **SFO** (United) or **Salt Lake** (Delta).
- **Money saver:** drive ~3 hr to PDX for the Alaska nonstop. Compare the
  RDM fare against PDX fare plus gas and parking.
- Expect the **highest fares** in the family from here.

### Portland, OR (PDX)
- **Alaska Airlines nonstop PDX–BOS** (multiple daily, including a red-eye).
- **Red-eye tip:** an overnight eastbound flight lands in Boston early in the
  morning, often for less money, and the drive to the Cape is easy before
  traffic builds.

### San Diego, CA (SAN)
- Nonstop BOS service has historically been on Alaska and JetBlue. Confirm
  for 2027. One-stop options on Delta, United, American and Southwest.

### Los Angeles, CA (LAX, plus LGB, BUR, SNA, ONT)
- **The most choice of anyone.** LAX–BOS nonstops on JetBlue, Delta,
  American, United and Alaska, so fares here tend to be the most competitive.
- Check Burbank and Orange County too if they're closer to home.

### Atlanta, GA (ATL)
- **Delta hub.** Many daily nonstops to BOS, plus PVD service. Compare BOS
  vs PVD, because PVD can be cheaper and much less hectic.
- Short flight (~2.5 hr), so a cheap early-morning departure on the travel
  day works well.

### Columbus, OH (CMH)
- Nonstop CMH–BOS on **Delta and American** per current listings. Southwest
  is worth checking for one-stop options, and it allows free changes.

## 5. When to buy

- **Flights are bookable now.** Airlines open sales about 330 days out, so
  July 2027 went on sale around early August 2026.
- **Set price alerts now** in Google Flights for each origin → BOS (and PVD)
  for the Wed Jun 30 → Wed Jul 7 window.
- For peak summer holiday travel, the best balance of price and seat choice
  is usually **2–4 months out (March–April 2027)**. Don't wait until the last
  month before a holiday week.
- **Book early with flexibility:** main-cabin fares on Alaska, Delta, United
  and American (not Basic Economy) and all Southwest fares have no change
  fees. If the price drops later, you can rebook and keep the difference as
  credit. That makes booking early nearly risk-free.
- Families flying from the same city should coordinate. Booking together
  makes shared rental cars and airport pickups easier.
